# HASHME Support

For operating instructions, consult the
[Operator's Manual](docs/HASHME_v1.2_Operators_Manual.pdf) and existing issues.

Non-sensitive, reproducible defects may be reported through the public issue
templates. For private support, security, licensing, or permission enquiries,
contact **hashme256@proton.me**.

Never attach confidential source files merely to demonstrate a hash result.
Use reproducible non-sensitive samples instead.
